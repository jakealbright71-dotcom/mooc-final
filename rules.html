Baseball Rules & How to Play

Learn the game step by step

Basic Rules of Baseball

1

Objective

Score more runs than the opposing team by hitting the ball and running around four bases to reach home plate.

2

Teams & Positions

Two teams of 9 players: pitcher, catcher, four infielders (1B, 2B, 3B, SS), and three outfielders (LF, CF, RF).

3

Game Structure

A game consists of 9 innings. Each team bats once per inning, trying to score runs while the other team plays defense.

4

Strikes & Balls

Three strikes = batter is out. Four balls = batter walks to first base. A strike is a pitch in the "strike zone" that the batter doesn't swing at, or any swing that misses.

5

Getting Outs

Three ways to get outs: strikeout (3 strikes), fly out (catch ball before it hits ground), or force out (defender with ball reaches base before runner).

6

Scoring Runs

A run scores when a player successfully circles all bases and touches home plate. Home runs (hit over outfield fence) score automatically.

How to Play - Step by Step

1

Pitching

The pitcher stands on the mound 60 feet 6 inches from home plate. They throw different types of pitches (fastball, curveball, slider) trying to get the batter out or induce weak contact.

2

Batting

The batter stands in the batter's box trying to hit the pitched ball. Good batting requires hand-eye coordination, timing, and understanding of different pitch types.

3

Base Running

After hitting the ball, the batter becomes a runner and must reach first base before the ball. Runners can advance on hits by teammates or steal bases when pitcher throws to home.

4

Fielding

Defensive players try to catch batted balls or retrieve them quickly to make outs. Infielders field ground balls, outfielders catch fly balls, and all fielders must make accurate throws.

5

Scoring

A runner scores by touching all four bases in order (1st, 2nd, 3rd, home) without being put out. Teams score multiple runs by having multiple runners circle the bases.

Baseball Rules Quiz

How many strikes does a batter get before being called out?

Answer Explanation: A batter receives three strikes before being called out. A strike can be: 1) a swing and miss, 2) a pitch in the strike zone that the batter doesn't swing at, or 3) a foul ball (except with two strikes). The "three strikes you're out" rule is fundamental to baseball.

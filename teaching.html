Activity 1: Wiffle Ball Game with Rules

🎮
20-30 minutes Whole class

What You Need:

Wiffle ball & bat 4 bases (or cones) Open space

Simple Steps:

1

Set up field - Use cones or markers for bases in gym or playground

2

Review rules - Quickly go over basic rules from rules.html page

3

Play game - Divide into two teams and play short innings

4

Discuss - After game, talk about what they learned about baseball

Learning Goals:

  • Understand basic baseball rules by playing
  • Learn teamwork and sportsmanship
  • Connect physical activity to sports history
  • Practice following game instructions

Teacher Tip: Use simplified rules - 3 strikes = out, hit past cones = run, everyone gets to bat

Activity 2: 5-Minute Player Presentation

🎤
1 class period Individual or pairs

What You Need:

Index cards Website access Timer

Simple Steps:

1

Choose player - Each student picks a baseball player from history

2

Research - Use website to find 3 facts about the player

3

Prepare - Write notes on index card (name, team, why important)

4

Present - Each student gives 5-minute talk about their player

Player Suggestions:

Jackie Robinson

First African American MLB player

Babe Ruth

Famous home run hitter

Roberto Clemente

Puerto Rican star, humanitarian

Ichiro Suzuki

Japanese star in MLB

Presentation Template:

"My player is [Name]. He played for [Team]. Three important facts are: 1) [Fact 1], 2) [Fact 2], 3) [Fact 3]. He is important because [Reason]."

Teacher Tip: Use timer to keep presentations short. Encourage students to speak clearly and make eye contact.
